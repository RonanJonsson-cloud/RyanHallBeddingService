## What Does Laundry Have To Do With AI?

RHBScheduler is an interactive scheduler that aids clients and entrepreneurs in picking mutually convenient drop-off and pick-up times. An AI assistant helps a client to choose drop-off and Pick-up times by analyzing a simple screenshot of their weekly schedule and comparing it to the entrepreneur's own screenshot as well as previously booked times.

**Core Question** How can AI aid new entrepreneurs in mundane and tedious tasks in order to effectively complement their business and streamline daily planning?

**Answer** This question can be answered in RHBScheduling's ability to seamlessly connect client to business across busy schedules.
