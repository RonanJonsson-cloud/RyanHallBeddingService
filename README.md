## What Does Laundry Have To Do With AI?

RHBScheduler is an interactive scheduler that aids clients and entrepreneurs in picking mutually convenient drop-off and pick-up times. An AI assistant helps a client to choose drop-off and Pick-up times by analyzing an input of their weekly schedule and comparing it to the entrepreneur's own schedule as well as previously booked times. This branch specifically submits customer chosen times as requests that automaticallyu are sent via email or text and must be confirmed by the entrepreneur.

**Core Question:** How can AI aid new entrepreneurs in mundane and tedious tasks in order to effectively complement their business and streamline daily planning?

**Answer:** This question can be answered in RHBScheduling's ability to seamlessly connect client to business across busy schedules.
